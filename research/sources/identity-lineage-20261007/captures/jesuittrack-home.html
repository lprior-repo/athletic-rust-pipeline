1986 - Eric Mastalir wins the 3200 at the California State Meet.  His 8:44.95 stood as the National HS Federation record until broken by Dathan Ritzenhein in 2000.

1986 - Mark Mastalir wins the State 1600 in 4:07.81.  A week earlier he edged brother Eric in 4:04.15, the fastest time in the country that year.

1998 - Mike Altieri takes second in the State 1600.
1998 - Altieri doubled back in the 3200 and placed 6th.  Three Marauders have made the podium in the 1600 and 3200 at the state meet:  Paul Thomas (1987), Michael Stember (1995), and Mike Altieri.
