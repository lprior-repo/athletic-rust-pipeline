  • Athletics

Built for Competition. Driven by Character.

With 14 Division I sports and a legacy of excellence, Jesuit Athletics challenges student-athletes to pursue their highest potential while developing leadership, sportsmanship, and resilience.

 

Sports by Season

We welcome students at every grade level to join in and be a Marauder student-athlete. 

Fall:  Cross Country*, Football*, Water Polo** 

Winter:  Basketball, Soccer, Wrestling* 

Spring:  Baseball, Golf**, Lacrosse, Rugby*, Swim and Dive*, Tennis**, Track and Field*, Volleyball 

*non-cut    **non-cut for lower levels
