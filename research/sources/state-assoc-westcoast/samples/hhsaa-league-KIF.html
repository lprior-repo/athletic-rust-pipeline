KIF
The Kauai Interscholastic Federation (KIF) was first organized in 1937 with two schools, Kauai High School and Waimea High School. In 1946 Kapaa High School opened and joined the KIF. The KIF now has eight member schools (Public, Private and Charter schools) that compete in 29 Varsity sports for Boys and Girls along with eight JV sports for Boys and Girls.

Member Schools (8)

  • Island School

  • Kanuikapono Public Charter School

  • Kapa‘a High School

  • Kauai Christian Academy

  • Kaua‘i High School

  • Kawaikini NCPCS

  • Ke Kula Niihau O Kekaha

  • Waimea High School
