THE BEST OF THE BEST: WV MIDDLE SCHOOL INVITATIONAL

Welcome to the inaugural The Best of The Best: WV Middle School Cross Country Invitational! The meet will be held on Friday, October 23rd at the Huntington YMCA Kennedy Center located on RT 2 in Huntington, WV.

When: Friday, October 23rd

Where: Huntington YMCA Kennedy Center

Cost:

$10 per individual (4 or fewer athletes per gender participating in the meet)

 $70 | 5-15 competitors per gender

$90 | 16-30 competitors per gender

$125 | 31+ competitors per gender 

$230 | Max per school (boys and girls combined)

 EXAMPLE: a team has 13 girls and 19 boys; they will pay $70 for the girls and $90 for the boys.

Awards: Top ten in each championship race will receive plaques, 11-30 will receive medals. 1st and 2nd place teams in the championship race will receive a trophy. Top 30 in open race will receive medals.

  • The meet will host a championship race as well as an open race (boys and girls combined).
  • For the championship race, a max of seven runners (per gender) can participate.
  • For the open race, there will be unlimited entries.
  • Schools with fewer than 5 competitors per gender may enter either the championship or open race.
  • Athletes who earn a medal will be given them as they finish the race. The top ten finishers in the championship races will be sent to the podium. 
  • Team awards will be given for the two championship races to the champion and runner-up. These awards will be given at the podium following the conclusion of the race.

Race times:

4:30 open race

5:00 girls championship

5:30 boys championship

 

Registration deadline: Tuesday, October 20th 5:00 PM

Final list of the BoB participants:  Wednesday, October 21st

BoB Date: Friday, October 23rd
