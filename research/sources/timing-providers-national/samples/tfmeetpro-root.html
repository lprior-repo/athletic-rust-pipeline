Track & Field Meet
Management Made Easy

Introducing MeetPro, the new track & field and cross country meet management application from DirectAthletics. MeetPro is an inexpensive and full-featured meet management solution designed to meet the needs of timers, meet directors, coaches, athletes and fans.

Web Ready

MeetPro is designed with the web in mind. Seamlessly integrated with DirectAthletics and TFRRS. Uploads live results via FTP or Amazon's S3. Imports entries in common formats.

Flexible & Powerful

MeetPro generates reports in PDF, HTML and Rich Text and exports data to Excel and CSV. Data can be imported from TFRRS, DirectAthletics, Semi-Colon Delimited files, TCL files, templates and existing meet databases.

Platform Neutral

MeetPro works the same in Windows and Mac, no emulation necessary.
