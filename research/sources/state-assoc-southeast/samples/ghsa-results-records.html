GHSA Results and Records from State Playoff Events

The following list contains links to all GHSA Results and Records from each year in the history of the GHSA that are currently available at the GHSA Office. These files were extracted from the GHSA Constitutions of the year following the year(s) in which the contests were held. Some files have title pages listing the contest years, some do not - but the dates listed below should be accurate. Click on the link to download the Results for that year. Some files are very large and may take a while to download (the size of the file is listed below the link). The files from 2003 to the present are digital copies. All other years are scanned images of the books we currently have on file (hence the much larger file sizes). We are interested in obtaining and scanning any GHSA Constitutions that are missing from this list. Please contact webmaster@ghsa.net for questions and comments or to relay any information on obtaining older GHSA Constitutions.

The GHSA wishes to thank Gilbert Head of the University of Georgia Hargrett Library for locating many of the GHSA Constitutions from 1909 to 1940.

A separate archive of GHSA Constitution and By-Laws is also available. Click here for GHSA Constitutions and By-Laws from Prior Years.
