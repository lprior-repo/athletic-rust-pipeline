Wisconsin Interscholastic Athletic Association

Inspiring champions for life.

The Wisconsin Interscholastic Athletic Association (WIAA) governs high school sports statewide, representing over 500 schools and nearly 200,000 student-athletes. The WIAA’s mission is to strive for excellence and consistent standards through education-based athletics.

Who We are

The Wisconsin Interscholastic Athletic Association (WIAA) governs high school sports statewide, representing over 500 schools nearly 200,000 student-athletes. It promotes education-based athletics that build character, fairness, and community.
