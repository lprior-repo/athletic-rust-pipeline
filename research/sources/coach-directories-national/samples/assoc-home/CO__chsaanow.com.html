Davis, Roberts-Uhlig to Present at Special Olympics Colorado State Conference

AURORA – Two of the Colorado High School Activities Associations staff members will be presenting at the second-annual Special Olympics Colorado State Conference at Arapahoe Community College this Friday. This year’s event theme is “Building Inclusive Communities” and CHSAA’s Association Commissioner Jenn Roberts-Uhlig and Assistant Commissioner Rashaan Davis will team up to share Strategies for Veteran Unified Schools Contacts. Davis and Roberts-Uhlig, who oversee unified esports and unified bowling, respectively, for CHSAA, will explore strategies for taking a high school’s Unified program to the next level. They will discuss ways to engage school leadership, district support, administrators and the student body to strengthen and grow a Unified program. Attendees will leave the 45-minute session with practical tools and a focused strategy to support the continued success of a Unified program. The Special Olympics Colorado Statewide Conference is an exciting event that brings together community coaches, educators, athletic directors, paraprofessionals and school administrators for more than two days of dynamic training, networking and professional development. The conference combines the Coaches Summit and Unified Champion Schools Conference, offering tailored sessions designed to elevate coaching expertise, enhance inclusive programming and strengthen Special Olympics initiatives across Colorado. In addition to sports-specific training, the Unified Champion Schools (UCS) Sessions will give schools access to tools and resources that will help start or enhance a Unified program at the school level. There will also be workshops and breakout sessions, networking opportunities and a coach appreciation night. The event runs this Thursday through Saturday, Sept. 24-26 and registration is still open.
