ABOUT THE IHSAA

The Iowa High School Athletic Association is a nonprofit organization that coordinates, regulates, and promotes high school and junior high boys’ sports and student activities in Iowa. The IHSAA offers free membership to all high schools recognized by the Iowa Department of Education.

UNIFIED ACTIVITIES PARTNERS

IHSAA works with related Iowa organizations like the Iowa High School Music Association, the Iowa Girls High School Athletic Union, and the Iowa High School Speech Association to provide and enrich student activities across Iowa.

These Unified Activities organizations and our proud sponsors share a goal in supporting Iowa high school students in programs that set students up for success post-graduation.

Iowa is the only state in the nation with separate athletic organizations by gender.
