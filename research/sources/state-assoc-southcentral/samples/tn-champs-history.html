Hall of Fame

The Tennessee Secondary School Athletic Association is a voluntary, nonprofit, self-supported organization, conceived by school people (teachers, principals, superintendents) and administered by individuals carefully chosen to conduct the program.

TSSAA was first organized in 1925 with the first state office being established in 1946. There are more students participating in TSSAA sports today than the total number of students in attendance at all junior and senior high schools in 1946 (110,000 vs 85,000).
