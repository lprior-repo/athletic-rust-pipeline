    September 08, 2026
    988 Reasons: Your reason is reason enough
    The Rhode Island Interscholastic League joins new RIIL-partner Horizon Healthcare Partners in recognizing the third annual 988 Day on September 8 (9/8), a national initiative led by the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA) to raise awareness of the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (988 Lifeline) and highlight the importance of prioritizing mental health and seeking help. 988 Day is a key part of the broader September focus on suicide prevention, mental health awareness, and recovery. This year’s 988 Day theme is “988 Reasons: Your reason is enough,” highlighting the many reasons people may need support, such as a difficult moment, a mental health challenge, or a concern for someone else. Our participation aims to remind those who are struggling that they are not alone. Anyone can call, text, or chat 988 anytime, 24/7, to connect with a skilled, caring, and non-judgmental 988 counselor, who will listen, help with safety planning when needed, and connect them with additional resources. Learn more about The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline at: hhpartners.org/988

    New Partner Alert: Horizon Healthcare Partners /RI's 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
    WARWICK, R.I. – [September 1, 2026] – The Rhode Island Interscholastic League (RIIL) is pleased to announce a new partnership with Horizon Healthcare Partners, operators of Rhode Island’s 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, to champion student-athlete mental health statewide. Through this collaboration, the RIIL will be better suited to meet the mental and emotional well-being of the entire RIIL community, which serves approximately 40,000 middle school and high school student-athletes across the state. Today’s youth are swimming in an endless sea of notifications, algorithms, and social stressors—making it easier than ever to feel isolated. 988 serves as a 24/7-365 open door to offer what youth and their families truly need in a crisis: a real, compassionate human connection when they’re unsure where to turn.     New Partner Alert: Rhode Island Student Loan Authority
    The Rhode Island Interscholastic League (RIIL) is pleased to announce a new partnership with the Rhode Island Student Loan Authority (RISLA). Through this collaboration, the RIIL and RISLA aim to expand access to college planning resources, financial aid guidance and financial literacy support for Rhode Island student-athletes and their families.     12th Annual RIIL Peanut Butter Express Challenge Leads to More Than 1,500-pound donation to RI Community Food Bank Smithfield High School leads collection effort Johnston brings in second-highest donation Gallagher M.S. is top participating middle sc
    The Rhode Island Interscholastic League’s 12th annual Peanut Butter Express Challenge generated more than 1,500 pounds of peanut butter for the Rhode Island Community Food Bank, continuing a yearly statewide student-led effort to support neighbors facing food insecurity. Smithfield Public Schools led the collection effort with Smithfield High School collecting the most peanut butter at 518.5 pounds and Gallagher Middle School in Smithfield leading all middle schools and finishing third overall with 317 pounds. Johnston High School collected the second-highest amount of peanut butter among all schools with close to 330 pounds.
