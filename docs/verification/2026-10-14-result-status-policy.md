# Explicit result-status contradictions and publication policy 18

Date: 2026-10-14 UTC. Integrated result-status repair; final combined evidence in progress.

## Contract

A source's explicit DQ, DNF, DNS, NH, FOUL, NM, NT or SCR statement contradicts a numeric
mark with the same natural result identity. Both arrival orders must retain the original
observations, union their evidence and record a scalar conflict. A contradictory result
context is ineligible for a best-mark winner. A different eligible retained result can win;
when all candidates are ineligible the published mark remains blank. This is conservative
contradiction handling, not a claim to have adjudicated a withdrawal or correction.

The domain owns the shared ten-token borrowed result-status classification. Its source consumers
retain their trim, ASCII-case-insensitive recognition, exact trimmed token and precedence.
X and -- remain ambiguous missing-result placeholders; they do not assert an explicit
terminal outcome. Unrecognized Raw values retain existing numeric-refinement behavior.
No new serialized Mark representation or historical raw-observation rewrite is introduced.
The older HyTek eight-token parser contract is unchanged; broadening its NT/-- recognition
requires separate source-specific qualification.

## Compatibility

Future dataset lineage, frozen-input semantic policy and publication recipes move from
17 to 18. Logical schema 3, frozen codec/token 3, manifest codec 2, Store schema 2 and
key format 1 are unchanged by this result-status change. Previous policy artifacts are
retained byte-for-byte and refused as current semantic inputs. Their original receipts,
leases, identities and roots cannot certify a policy-18 publication. Fresh recapture and
new publication use new policy-bound identities. No automatic artifact upgrade is allowed.

Current-policy test expectations advance deliberately. Historical policy-16 fixtures remain;
policy-17 preservation is added to archive, root-state and cancellation matrices. The
publication cutover exercises both policy 16 and 17, retaining all old artifact bytes and
receipts across publishing and Store reopen. The exact policy-17 token spelling remains
explicitly checked alongside the new token and must have a different digest.
